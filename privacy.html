Privacy Policy – Partituras YouTube

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Partituras YouTube is a private tool used only by its owner, the arranger Gabriel Altali, to upload demonstration videos of his own sheet music arrangements to his own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

Through the YouTube Data API, the tool uploads videos, sets their thumbnails and posts a comment on those videos. It acts only on the owner's channel, with the owner's authorization.

Data stored

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer. The tool keeps a local record of the IDs and links of the videos it uploaded. It does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person, and it has no other users.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
